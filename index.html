Customize Your Pet Necklace

Create a personalized necklace featuring your beloved pet!

To order a custom pet necklace, please send us a clear, close-up photo of your pet's face. The clearer the photo, the more detailed and accurate we can make your necklace charm.

Photo Requirements:

  • Clear, close-up photo of your pet's face
  • Good lighting (natural light is best)
  • Direct angle (looking at the camera)
  • High resolution image

Once you share your pet's photo, our team will create a beautiful custom necklace charm. Contact us with your photo to get started!
